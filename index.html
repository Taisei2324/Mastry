Chapter three

Two worlds.
One water.

Mastic is a living resin — it weeps from the bark of the Pistacia lentiscus tree, found only on the Greek island of Chios. For over 2,500 years it has been prized as a medicine, a perfume, and a food. Today, it's the soul of Mastry.

We carry that resin across the world to Japan, where our water is drawn from mountain springs and bottled with the precision Japan is known for. The result is a sparkling water unlike anything else in existence.
