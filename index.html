Χίος — Greece

The tears of Chios

When the bark of the mastic tree is scored, it weeps slow drops of resin that harden into fragrant crystals — the islanders call them tears. Harvested by hand in the groves of southern Chios since antiquity, mastic tastes like nothing else: cool pine, cedar, a whisper of the sea.

日本 — Japan

The craft of clarity

Soft Japanese water, fine persistent carbonation, and the restraint to leave things out — no sugar, no sweeteners, nothing that clouds the glass. Each bottle is finished in Japan, its label painted in the quiet tradition of sumi-e landscape.

The ritual — 作法

Make it a moment.

01

Chill, never freeze

Four degrees. Cold enough to tighten the bubbles, warm enough that the resin still speaks.

02

Pour against the glass

Tilt and pour slowly down the side. The carbonation is fine — treat it like something rationed.

03

Pair across the map

Original with sashimi or mezze. Yuzu with anything fried. Ume before dinner, Hinoki after it.
