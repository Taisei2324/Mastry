Our Story

Born in Osaka,
Loved Worldwide

Umami Udon started as a tiny 8-seat counter in the Namba district. Our founder, Kenji Tanaka, learned the art of hand-pulled noodles from his grandmother, who ran a small udon-ya for 40 years.

Today we serve hundreds of guests daily, but every bowl is still made with the same patience and care — dashi simmered for 12 hours, noodles pulled fresh every morning, toppings sourced from local farms.
