Chapter three

Two worlds.
One water.

Mastry was born from a single question: what happens when the rarest natural resin on earth meets the world's most refined water culture?

Mastic — harvested only on the Greek island of Chios — has been prized for 2,500 years for its purity and therapeutic properties. We carry it to Japan, where our water is sourced from pristine mountain springs, carbonated, and bottled with obsessive care.

The result is something that has never existed before.
